Contact

Bring us a problem.

Don't send us a job title. Don't send us a list of technologies. Bring us something you don't understand, something that keeps failing, or something you are trying to design.

A good problem looks like…

  • “I don’t understand why this system behaves this way.”
  • “This keeps failing and I can’t find the cause.”
  • “I’m designing something and I’m not sure it’s the right shape.”
  • “AI generated this and I can’t tell if I should trust it.”

Short is fine. Precise is better. You'll hear back from a person.
